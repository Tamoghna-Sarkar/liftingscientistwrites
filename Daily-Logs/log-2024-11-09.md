# Daily Log - 09 November 2024 (written as of Dec 8)

## Lot's of ongoing work, finals, submissions - HENCE DELAYED


- **The Event**:  
  I had the privilege of attending the **GENZERO24 Autonomous Drone Challenge** in **Abu Dhabi**, hosted by the **Technology Innovation Institute (TII)** and staying at the luxurious **Hilton Yas Islands**. It was an honor to present the **SHIELD proposal**, a project aimed at enabling real-time threat detection and mitigation for autonomous drone swarms using Edge AI and Federated Learning.  

- **The Presentation**:  
  - Standing before a room of distinguished professors and researchers was humbling and rewarding. Their curiosity and insightful questions brought new dimensions to my work, reminding me that ideas, like humans, grow best when exposed to diverse perspectives.  
  - Moments like these underline the beauty of academia—a collective journey of discovery where every critique, every question, is a step toward refinement and progress.

    > “Your seeing is equivalent to us seeing. Don’t worry.”

- **On Ambition and Contentment**:  
  - Standing atop the Burj Khalifa, I reflected on the grandeur of human achievement and the weight it carries. Ambition is a gift, but it must be tempered with humility. As we reach for the skies, we mustn’t forget the grounding warmth of human connection.

  - The event and travel reaffirmed that growth happens in moments of discomfort—when we present our work to new audiences, meet unfamiliar faces, or confront solitude.
